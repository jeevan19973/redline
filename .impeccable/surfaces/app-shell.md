# Surface brief: app shell (signed-in)

Scope: the frame every signed-in screen lives in. Brief only; no screen is built yet. Tickets 01 to 12 and 17 to 18 build what it holds.

Mode: Operate. Expression never obscures the task, the state or a familiar control. The shell takes the Depth of Reach world's type, palette, material accents and one signature move; navigation and controls stay standard web components.

Audience: an invited Signer at a desk on a laptop, reading a report beside their own copy of the document. Visits are occasional and focused: one deal, a few Drafts, then gone for weeks. The phone must work but is not primary.

What the frame holds:
- Add a Draft: upload a text-based PDF, Word or plain-text file, or paste the document's text. Plain refusals for scanned files and unsupported types. Analysis in progress and failure with retry.
- The report on one Draft: plain-English summary; ranked Risk flags, Dangerous before Caution, each with its Source sentences, Reading, and a Counter-offer or a take-it-or-leave-it label; the Clean verdict with the list of what was checked; the guaranty gap; the scope stamp; the Red lines the report ran against and a re-run.
- The extracted text, with every cited sentence findable in place.
- The question box: answers that quote their sentences, or a plain "the document does not say".
- The Signer's Red lines: the default catalog read-only, their own editable.
- The library: every Draft, newest first; open and delete.
- Remaining analyses and questions from the one-time limit.

Layout intent: a persistent left rail (Library, Red lines, Add a Draft, remaining limit, Sign out); the report as two panes on desktop, the Draft's text on the left and the ranked flags on the right, linked both ways; one pane with a toggle on narrow screens.

Signature move carried from the landing page: every flag shows its reach on a small depth gauge beside its severity label, the thermocline marking where the business ends. Order on screen stays Dangerous first; depth is shown, never used to push Dangerous down the page.

States the frame must design for: empty library, analysis in progress, analysis failed, a Clean verdict, a report with only Caution flags, a guaranty gap, a Non-negotiable clause, the question box's "does not say", limit reached, scanned-file refusal, and a deleted Draft.

Constraints: Confidence hidden behind a switch until calibration passes. Paper for reading surfaces: the Draft text and every flag sit on paper, ink with underlines; the deep ink-blue is chrome and ground only. Red only on Dangerous; Clean verdict neutral. WCAG 2.2 AA; keyboard operable throughout. US English through the humanizer skill.

Unresolved: whether the rail collapses on laptop widths; how the question box sits beside the two panes (third column, drawer or below the flags); how many Drafts the library shows before it needs search.
